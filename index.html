Privacy Policy & Data Safety

This Privacy Policy describes how My Kegel Partner handles user data, privacy, and security in compliance with Google Play Store and Apple App Store policies.

Last Updated: 1 January 2026

My Kegel Partner (“App”, “Application”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a wellness and fitness application developed by CRESCENDO, designed to guide users through Kegel (pelvic floor) exercises that support muscle strength, endurance, and general health awareness.

1. Purpose of the Application

My Kegel Partner focuses on guided Kegel exercises intended to support pelvic floor muscle strength, endurance, and overall wellness.

The Application is designed for fitness and self-care purposes only and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition.

Users should consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any exercise routine if they have health concerns.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

The App does not require account creation and does not collect personal identifiers such as name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

2.2 Exercise & Wellness Data

Users may voluntarily generate or enter data including:

  • Kegel exercise sessions and completion status
  • Workout duration, repetitions, and streaks
  • Reminder preferences

This data:

  • Is stored locally on the user’s device
  • Is not transmitted to our servers
  • Is not accessed, shared, or sold by us

2.3 Automatically Collected Technical Data

Limited non-identifiable technical data may be collected, such as:

  • App version and build number
  • Device type and operating system
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

3. Permissions Usage

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications: To send exercise reminders
  • Vibration / Sound: For guided exercise cues

No sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, camera, or microphone are used.

4. Data Storage & Retention

All user data is stored locally on the device. We do not operate cloud storage, servers, or external databases for user data.

Users may delete all stored data by clearing app data or uninstalling the App.

5. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or share user data with advertisers or third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may integrate trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Play Services
  • Apple App Store Services
  • Crash reporting or analytics tools (if enabled)

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

7. Security Measures

We use reasonable security practices appropriate for a local-storage application, including OS-level sandboxing.

Users are responsible for securing their device using PINs, biometrics, and system updates.

8. Children’s Privacy

My Kegel Partner is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. User Rights

Since all data remains on the user’s device, users retain full control over their data. Uninstalling the App permanently removes all stored information.

10. Limitation of Liability

The Application is provided “as is”. CRESCENDO disclaims liability for health outcomes, exercise results, or data loss.
